# Lab

Experiments and working prototypes by [Gustav Arnetz](https://arnetz.design), independent principal product designer for AI and consumer products. Most of it is designed and built with Claude Code.

Each folder is one experiment you can run on its own. The write-ups live at [arnetz.design/lab](https://arnetz.design/lab).

| Experiment | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Ask me about my work](ask-me-about-my-work) | A voice prototype: ask a portfolio a question out loud and hear the answer, powered by Claude. |
| [Voice orbs](voice-orbs) | Eight visual directions for a voice assistant's presence, all reacting to the same conversation moments. |
| [Shaders for interfaces](interface-shaders) | Ten shaders placed in real screens (sign in, a trail map, a camera, a shutter, a payment) and driven by their states, four of them about light. |
| [Design rules](design-rules) | The rules my portfolio is built to, as a Claude skill you can download, with a real before and after. |
| [Research to prototype: a fitness study](fitness-study) | Mobbin research on 32 screens, a moodboard, a visual language, six guidelines, and a prototype that follows them. |
| [Darkroom](darkroom) | Six shaders that work on real photographs: relight a room, film halation, tilt-shift, film, sun rays and golden hour. Try them on your own photo. |
| [The Sunken Crypt](sunken-crypt) | A dark action RPG in the spirit of Diablo 1, made with Claude Code in Godot: how it was made, and the first browser prototype to play. |
| [Contour map of anywhere](contour-map) | Search any place and see it as a live topographic map drawn from open elevation data. Point to light the line at that height, and turn the sun. |
| [Photo trail](photo-trail) | Photos from a hike placed on the map by the location and time saved in them, then the day replays with each photo at its spot. Photos stay in your browser. |
| [Turnaround time](turnaround-time) | From your pace, the climb ahead and the daylight left: when to turn back, and how sure that is. A study in designing for uncertainty. |
| [Ask the map](ask-the-map) | Ask for a walk in plain words and get real marked trails on a contour map, with what the map measured kept apart from Claude's guesses. |
| [Playlist from a photo](playlist-from-a-photo) | Claude reads a photo's light and mood and picks ten songs that sound the way it looks, with previews to play. |
