Voice orbs

In a voice conversation the screen has one job: show whose turn it is. These are eight visual directions for that, all reacting to the same moments. Pick a moment, play a short conversation, or try your microphone, and tap any look to see it large.

Without a microphone, voice is simulated. With one, the orbs follow your voice level on this page only. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Eight looks

What every look follows

  • Colour says whose turn it isOne colour at rest, a cooler one while you talk and a deeper one while it talks. You can tell at a glance without reading a word.
  • Each moment moves differentlyListening follows your voice. A pause slows to breathing. Thinking gathers inward. Speaking glows from the centre. Mic off goes grey and nearly still.
  • Breath, not bounceAt rest it breathes four seconds in and six out. Every change eases over about 800ms, so nothing snaps or jitters.
  • Calm when askedWith reduced motion on, the shapes hold still and each moment shows through light and colour alone.
