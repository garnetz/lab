Playlist from a photo

Drop in a photo. Claude reads it the way a photographer would, the light, the colour, the time of day, and picks ten songs that sound the way it looks. Press play to hear them.

Your photo goes to Claude to read its mood. It isn’t stored. Previews come from Apple Music.
