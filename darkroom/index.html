Darkroom

Six shaders that work on real photographs, the way a lens, a film stock or a darkroom would. Move a light across a room, let neon bleed into the film, turn a coastline into a model, or move the sun. Each one runs live on the photo, and you can hold to see the original.

Your photo stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How they work

  • The photo is the inputEach shader reads the photo, not a made-up scene. What it does depends on what’s in the picture: where the light is, where the edges are.
  • Borrowed from photographyEvery effect has a real-world cause: a light you can move, light scattering in film, a tilted lens, a film stock, haze, the time of day.
  • One pass, liveEach runs in a single pass on the graphics card, as you move, and stops drawing when it’s off screen.
