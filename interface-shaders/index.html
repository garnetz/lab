Shaders for interfaces

Ten shaders, each placed in a real screen and driven by what the screen is doing. A background calms down while you type. A skeleton turns into the rows it was holding a place for. And four that play with light the way a photographer does: low sun, out-of-focus lights, a light leak. Try the states, use the screens, or play each one through.

What every study follows

  • A shader serves a momentEach one exists for a state of the screen: waiting, typing, confirming, failing. It is never just wallpaper.
  • The words stay readableWhen text sits on top, the shader calms and darkens behind it. Content wins over effect.
  • States ease, never cutEvery change blends over about 0.8 seconds with the same easing curve as the rest of the interface.
  • Calm when askedWith reduced motion on, nothing moves on its own. A state change still shows, and a tap still gets its answer.
