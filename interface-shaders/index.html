Shaders for interfaces

Six small shaders an interface could actually use: a background, a glass panel, a light that follows you, a loading state, a texture and a response to touch. Each one runs live on the GPU. Move over them, tap them, turn the dials, and open the code underneath.

What every study follows

  • Quiet by defaultSlow, low contrast and dark, so the content on top stays the point. Colour comes from one accent.
  • Answer peopleThe pointer, a tap or a press changes something. A surface that reacts feels like a material, not a video.
  • Cheap to runOne pass each, no textures, and nothing draws while it is off screen.
  • Calm when askedWith reduced motion on, nothing moves on its own. A tap still gets its answer.
