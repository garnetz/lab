Photo trail

Drop in the photos from a hike. Each one goes on the map where it was taken, using the location and time your phone or camera saved in it, and the day plays back with every photo at its spot.

Your photos stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. JPEG photos work best; most phones can export them. A GPX track from a watch or app adds the real path and places photos that have a time but no location.

How it works

  • The photo knowsMost phones save where and when a photo was taken inside the file. The page reads that straight from the file, in your browser, before showing anything.
  • Time fills the gapsCameras without GPS still keep the time. With a GPX track, each of those photos goes where you were at that moment. If the camera clock was off, shift it until the photos land right.
  • Honest about missesA photo with no location and no matching time isn't guessed onto the map. It's listed with the reason, so you know what to fix.
  • Private by designWhere you were and when is personal. Nothing is uploaded or stored. Close the tab and it's gone. Only the map tiles come from the internet.
