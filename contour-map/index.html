Contour map of anywhere

Search a place and see it as a topographic map, drawn live from real elevation data. Point at the map and the line at that height lights up, so you can follow it around the mountain. Turn the sun to read the shape of the land.

Dials

How it works

  • Real groundThe heights come from open elevation tiles that cover the whole planet, sharper where countries have published detailed surveys. They're colour-coded images, decoded into metres in your browser.
  • One shader draws it allFor every pixel, the shader finds the height and draws a line where it crosses a round number. Every fifth line is thicker, like on a paper map. Nothing is drawn ahead of time.
  • Lines you can readThe spacing follows what's in view. A gentle valley gets a line every 5 or 10 metres and a big mountain every 100, so the map never turns into noise or goes blank.
  • Light as a reading toolShading shows which slopes face the sun. Turn it to see a ridge from another side, or warm it to see the land the way it looks at golden hour.
