From the car to the mountain

  • Two-second glancesLike a dashboard, each card answers one question in one big number: how far, how long until dark, where am I. Everything else is smaller and can wait.
  • Made for glovesTargets are at least 72 points tall and there’s nothing to pinch, swipe or hold. Tap anywhere on a card for the next one. Night turns it red, to keep your eyes used to the dark.
  • Packed, not streamedWhile you still have signal, the route, its heights, the sunset and a picture of the map are worked out once and kept on the phone. After that it needs no network at all.
  • Easy on the batteryBlack screen, no animation, and a battery saver that asks for your position once a minute instead of all the time, and says how old the last fix is. Your position stays on the phone.
