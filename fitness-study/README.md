# A calmer fitness app

A design study in four steps, made with Claude Code: research, moodboard, guidelines and a working prototype.

1. **Research.** Four searches on [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com), eight iOS screens each: daily activity summaries, readiness and recovery scores, screens during a workout, and the screen right after. The apps include Apple Fitness, Garmin Connect, Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Visible, Gentler Streak, Peloton, Runna and Hevy.
2. **Moodboard.** Eight patterns that kept showing up, drawn as simple sketches with links to the screens on Mobbin. No screenshots are stored here; the screens belong to their makers.
3. **Guidelines.** Six rules for a calmer fitness app:
   - Say it in words first.
   - Compare to your usual, not to a goal.
   - Three reasons, in plain rows.
   - Time, now, next.
   - Rest is a state, not a wait.
   - End with a question, not confetti.
4. **Prototype.** Three screens (Today, Session, Done) for three sample mornings (Rested, Usual, Tired). The answer to "How did that feel?" changes what the next day suggests. All data is sample data.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. There are no libraries and no build step.

- `index.html` holds the page and the prototype's screens.
- `study.js` holds the moodboard tiles, the sample mornings and the session timer.
